My primary specialty is the development and integration of high-density cryogenic readout assemblies for use in cosmic microwave background (CMB) observatories. 

## [Simons Observatory](https://www.jenna-moore.com/research/simonsobservatory) 

The [Simons Observatory](https://www.simonsobservatory.org) is located at 5200m altitude atop Cerro Toco in the [Parque Astrónomico Atacama](https://astronomicalpark.cl) in the Atacama Desert in Chile. The Simons Observatory consists of three 0.5m Small Aperture Telescopes (SATs) and one 6m Large Aperture Telescope (LAT). The mix of small and large telescopes allows us to map the CMB at both large and small scales, facilitating a great deal of cosmological and astrophysical analysis.


The high altitude and extremely arid climate makes Cerro Toco a prime location for observing the cosmic microwave background.
